# Senior dog care schedule (dogs about 8 years and older)

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Feeding
Senior dogs eat 2 meals a day at regular times: breakfast at 07:30 and dinner at 17:30. Keep the times steady every day.

## Walks
Prefer shorter, more frequent walks. Plan 3 gentle walks of about 15 minutes a day, daily: 08:00, 13:00 and 18:00. Keep the pace easy.

## Check-ups
Schedule vet check-ups twice a year, about six months apart, with high priority.

## Rest
Leave quiet time between activities. Avoid packing walks and play close together in the schedule.
