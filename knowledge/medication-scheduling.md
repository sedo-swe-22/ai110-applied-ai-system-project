# Scheduling reminders for medication

> This file is only about *when* to remind the owner. It never says what to give or how much. Follow your vet's instructions for that.

## Daily reminders
Create a daily task at the same time every day, with high priority. Tie it to a meal time (for example 07:30 or 17:30), because a routine is easier to keep.

## Weekly and monthly treatments
For treatments given once a week, create a weekly task on the same weekday. For monthly treatments (such as flea or heartworm prevention), pick a date that is easy to remember, such as the 1st, and create a recurring reminder.

## Avoiding clashes
Do not put a medication reminder at the same time as a walk or grooming task. Move the other task instead.
