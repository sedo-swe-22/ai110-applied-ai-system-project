# Puppy care schedule (dogs under about 6 months)

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Feeding
Puppies eat 3 meals a day, spaced through the day: breakfast at 07:30, lunch at 12:30 and dinner at 17:30. Schedule all three as daily tasks.

## Walks and potty breaks
Puppies need several short outings rather than one long walk. Plan 4 short walks of 10 to 15 minutes a day, daily: 08:00, 13:00, 18:00 and a last potty break at 21:30.

## Play and training
Plan 2 short play or training sessions of about 10 minutes, daily: 10:30 and 15:30. Short and frequent works better than one long session.

## Vet visits
New puppies have a series of vet visits in their first months. Schedule the first visit soon after the puppy arrives and ask the clinic for the follow-up dates. Mark vet visits as high priority.
