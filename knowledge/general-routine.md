# Building a daily routine

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Spacing tasks
Keep at least 15 minutes between tasks for the same pet. Give feeding, walks and play their own time slots so nothing clashes.

## Consistency
Use the same times every day. Pets settle into a routine quickly, and daily tasks are the easiest way to keep it.

## New pets
For a new pet, ask the species and age (puppy, kitten, adult or senior) first, because meal counts and walk lengths depend on them. Use the matching care schedule for the species and life stage.

## Priorities
Use high priority for vet visits and medication reminders, medium for feeding and walks, and low for grooming.
