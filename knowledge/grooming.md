# Grooming schedule

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Brushing
Short-coated dogs and cats: brush once a week, as a weekly task. Long-coated or double-coated dogs (for example Shiba Inu): brush 3 times a week.

## Nails
Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks. Schedule it as a task and move it forward if the pet's nails click on the floor.

## Baths
Bathe dogs every 4 to 8 weeks. Most cats rarely need a bath. Do not schedule baths more often than every 4 weeks unless the vet says so.

## Professional grooming
For breeds that need clipping, book a groomer every 6 to 8 weeks and schedule the appointment as a "once" task with low priority.
