# Adult cat care schedule

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Feeding
Adult cats eat 2 meals a day: breakfast at 07:30 and dinner at 18:00. Schedule both as daily tasks.

## Play
Plan 2 play sessions of about 15 minutes a day, daily: 08:30 and 19:00.

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box once a day, daily, at 20:00. Change the litter fully once a week.

## Check-ups
Schedule a routine vet check-up once a year, as a "once" task, with high priority.
