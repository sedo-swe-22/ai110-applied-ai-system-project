# Adult dog care schedule

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Feeding
Adult dogs eat 2 meals a day: breakfast at 07:30 and dinner at 17:30. Schedule both as daily tasks.

## Walks
Plan 2 walks of about 30 minutes a day, daily: a morning walk at 08:00 and an evening walk at 18:30. Leave about 30 minutes after a meal before a long, energetic walk.

## Play and training
One play or training session of 15 to 20 minutes a day, for example at 16:00, keeps most adult dogs content.

## Check-ups
Schedule a routine vet check-up once a year, as a "once" task, with high priority.
