# Small pets: rabbits, guinea pigs and hamsters

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Daily care
Check fresh food and water twice a day, daily: 08:00 and 18:00. Rabbits and guinea pigs also need fresh hay available all day.

## Cage cleaning
Spot-clean the cage daily at 18:30. Do a full clean once a week, as a weekly task.

## Out-of-cage time
Rabbits and guinea pigs need 1 hour of supervised out-of-cage time a day, daily, for example at 17:00. Hamsters are most active in the evening, so use 19:30.
