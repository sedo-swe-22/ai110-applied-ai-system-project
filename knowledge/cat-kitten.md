# Kitten care schedule (cats under about 1 year)

> Sample scheduling guidance written for this project. It is general, not veterinary advice.

## Feeding
Kittens eat 4 small meals a day: 07:00, 11:30, 16:00 and 20:00. Schedule all four as daily tasks.

## Play
Plan 3 play sessions of about 10 minutes a day, daily: 09:00, 14:00 and 19:00. Play helps kittens burn energy and learn.

## Litter box
Check and scoop the litter box twice a day, daily, at 08:00 and 20:30.

## Vet visits
Kittens have a series of early vet visits. Schedule the first soon after arrival and ask the clinic for the follow-up dates. Mark them high priority.
